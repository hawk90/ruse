**Method.**
- `machine` axes: of the 67, five have an `automated_by` checker: `RA-ARCH-003` (spec-validate),
  `RA-ARCH-007` and `RA-ARCH-008` (dependency-check), `RA-ARCH-040` (design_code) and `RA-STRUCT-005`
  (ratchet). All five were run. Every other `machine` axis is marked **unwired**; its verdict comes from a
  measurement or gate run for this report.
- Structure figures come from a new throwaway script, run on `83ba5e23` and on the v0.2.2 pin `c62303c0`
  (`git archive` into separate scratch directories). It is not committed. Its rules are the v0.2.1 and v0.2.2
  reports':
  - It reads `crates/*/src` and `apps/*/src`.
  - It blanks comments and string and char literals, and measures a function from its `fn` line to its
    closing brace.
  - It excludes the generated `case_tables.rs`, test files (`tests.rs`, `*_tests.rs`, anything under a
    `tests/` directory), and the items inside `#[cfg(test)]` and `#[cfg(all(test, …))]` modules and
    functions.
  - On `c62303c0` it reproduces every v0.2.2 figure: 342 files, 1 file over 1,000 lines, 4 functions
    over 150, 49 over 100, 0 `use super::*;`, largest test file 2,287 lines, depth 4.
  - Like v0.2.2's script, it does not exempt `EmacsProfile::new` or `input/set_cmd/table.rs`, so its counts
    are one function and one file higher than `gov ratchet`'s.
- Path freshness comes from a second throwaway script over the v0.2.2 scope (`docs/` without
  `docs/reviews/`, `spec/`, `AGENTS.md`, `CONTRIBUTING.md`, `README.md`). It reproduces v0.2.2's `path::name`
  figures (58, 1 stale) but not its path count: it finds 474 path references at `c62303c0` where v0.2.2
  found 528, because its extraction rule differs. So it is compared only with itself on both pins.
- Parity figures come from `parity_compare` at `83ba5e23`. The `c62303c0` figures are the v0.2.2 report's,
  and its allowed-divergence list was read with `git show c62303c0:tests/parity/vim/known-divergences.txt`.
- `llm` and `mixed` axes: assessed by reading the code at the cited lines.
- `manual` axes: marked **needs maintainer**, with what to look at.
- A verdict without evidence is not a pass.
- Security-relevant findings are withheld: their rows say only "details reported privately". This
  report has none.
- "Moved" compares with `docs/reviews/2026-11-23-v0.2.2.md`. "new" means the axis was not in that report.

**Moved since v0.2.2:** of the 59 axes in both reports, 2 went from `partial` to `pass`: `RA-INPUT-018`
(no `.` divergence is left) and `RA-INPUT-019` (the three open mode-transition bugs are fixed). One went
from `pass` to `partial`: `RA-STRUCT-011`. v0.2.3 gave Vim marks a second stored form, so mark positions
are now kept two ways (Top risks 2). `RA-MAINT-005` stays `partial` but got worse (`-`). Of the 8 new axes,
5 are `pass` and 3 `partial`. Each of the 3 has the same verdict as when it was last assessed.

**Top risks**

1. **Most of the allowed divergences that are left have no open issue.**
   - The list went from 145 to 23 entries. 12 of the 23 are held by no open issue. Only one entry
     (`i1259_chain_undo_inside`, #639) names an open issue at all.
   - 7 of the 12 are real behaviour differences:
     - the Visual-block `$` cursor after `ge` / `<BS>` (`vb_dollar_ge_y`, `vb_dollar_bs_y`);
     - marks after `:normal` typeahead (`i1307_normal_ixy`, `i1307_normal_ghl`, `i1307_normal_R`);
     - `'<`/`'>` past the buffer's end (`v_dollar_D_to_eol_column`);
     - `'>` after a `gq` join (`v_gq_joins_short`).
   - 2 are the entries #1583 already asks issues for (`sc_mark_last_change_exact`, `ik_js_brace_object_value`).
   - 3 are labelled oracle artifacts, not divergences: `cm_insert_typed_run` and the two
     `vb_shift_right_*` stale-`mode i` entries. For `cm_insert_typed_run`, the claim that interactive nvim
     puts `'.` on the last typed char (the comment above it in the list) is written down, but no fixture
     captures it: the oracle feeds keys in one batch (`tools/parity/oracle.py:97`).
   - #1583 (open, v0.2.5) proposes to fail an entry without an open issue. As written, that rule would also
     fail the 3 oracle artifacts, which no issue can fix.
2. **Mark positions now have two stored forms, and the change-mark rule is written only in PRs.**
   - #1608 keeps the named marks, `'^` and `'<`/`'>` as `Mark { at, past }`, a raw column beside the offset
     (`crates/core/src/editor/mod.rs:294`, `Tracked::past` at `anchor.rs:560`).
   - The change marks `'[ '] '.` stay clamped byte offsets. A `'.` on the line past the end is kept as a
     separate flag, `View::dot_past_end` (`editor/mod.rs:217`, set at `commit.rs:146`, read at `:243` and
     `planner/marks.rs:195`). #1615 is open to give `'[ ']` the raw column. It does not mention `'.` or the flag.
   - #1302 (3 PRs) and #1303 port nvim's per-command rules for `'[ '] '.`. Those rules are written in the PR
     bodies and code comments (`planner/op_marks.rs:1-4`, `editor/action.rs:174-198`). The design doc still
     says only "bounds of last changed/yanked text" (`docs/design/positions-history.md:392`). #1608 did
     update `anchor-store.md` and `positions-history.md` for the raw columns.
3. **Function and file sizes grow again in fix PRs, under the gate.**
   - Functions over 100 lines went from 49 to 53. `planner/marks.rs::plan` is at 150, one line under the
     `fn_over_150` limit. Files over 800 lines went from 14 to 16, and `planner/mod.rs` is at 950.
   - `gov ratchet` passes (3/3, 0/0) because nothing new crossed 150 or 1,000 lines. Nothing guards the
     written 100/800 targets (`size-ceilings.md:25-26`).
   - This is the pattern v0.2.2 found with `incr_number` (Top risks 3). #1584 holds only the three functions
     already over 150.
4. **A replayed trace can change what it does, and nothing detects it.**
   - #1607 dropped the forced undo break before each Ex edit, so the edits of a `|`-chained line now form
     one undo step. Its PR says there is no trace format change. That holds for the recording.
   - But a `u` after a chained line in a trace recorded before #1607 now undoes the whole chain, not only
     the last command. This report reasons that from the change; it did not replay such a trace.
   - #1603 added `wrapscan` to the options a trace starts with (`ex_cmd/codec.rs:112, :548`).
   - The result hash that would catch a different outcome (#1264) moved from v0.2.3 to v0.2.5 when the
     milestone's work started.
5. **Parallel work overloaded the machine, and the verify path did not hold up under that load.**
   - Up to 7 fix agents ran at once, and the load average reached about 55.
   - The pre-push `verify --full` then took long enough that SSH pushes were dropped.
   - `resource_bounds` failed inside it and passed alone (#1619). Its bound is wall-clock time,
     `Duration::from_secs(10)` (`apps/tui/tests/resource_bounds.rs:16`).
   - The work changed course mid-milestone to local commits and serial merges by the maintainer session
     (Milestone summary). No repo document describes that flow.

**Premises corrected by the oracle.** Several issue premises were wrong, and capturing nvim first caught each
one before the fix:
- #1305: "`'<` `'>` cover whole lines" was wrong. nvim keeps the columns (#1602 body).
- #1307: the bug needs no failed key. Even `:normal vl` ended in Normal (#1598 body).
- #1577: filed as unverified. It was real (#1594 body).
- #1259: the assumed trace change and RFC were not needed (#1607 body).
- One premise came from the maintainer session itself, not from an issue: that the oracle's Insert `'.`
  shows nvim's interactive behaviour. A fix agent found it wrong, and #1611 lists `cm_insert_typed_run` as
  an oracle typeahead artifact.

**How the work ran.** The milestone took 8 hours of merges (#1591 merged 02:11 UTC, #1611 at 10:13 UTC).
- **Parallel phase.** Up to 7 agents worked in parallel worktrees at first. Several PRs were open at once:
  #1596, #1598, #1599, #1600 and #1601 were all open at 04:45.
- **The load.** The load average reached about 55 (#1619), and the long pre-push `verify --full` let SSH
  pushes drop.
- **Serial phase.** From then on, agents committed locally. The main session merged one PR at a time with a
  scripted flow: merge, regenerate the corpus, check, run `parity_compare`. From #1602 on, each PR was opened
  and merged within 5 to 8 minutes, one at a time (PR timestamps). That script lives in the session, not in
  the repo.

fixes. The eighth is the parity harness: `parity_compare.rs` now reads a mark's raw column (`view.mark_raw`, #1608). The
ignored count stayed at 5.

`tools/parity/oracle.py` (5,954 → 6,581 lines); #1592 used existing fixtures.

**Moved out:** the 6 issues above, at the start. Follow-ups filed during the milestone: #1612-#1618
