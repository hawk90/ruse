**Method.**
- `machine` axes: of the 59, five have an `automated_by` checker: `RA-ARCH-003` (spec-validate),
  `RA-ARCH-007` and `RA-ARCH-008` (dependency-check), `RA-ARCH-040` (design_code) and, new in v0.2.2,
  `RA-STRUCT-005` (ratchet, #1512). All five were run. Every other `machine` axis is marked **unwired**; its
  verdict comes from a measurement or gate run for this report.
- Structure figures come from a throwaway script, run on `c62303c0` and on the v0.2.1 pin `cc81e245`
  (`git archive` into separate scratch directories). It is not committed. Its rules are the v0.2.1 report's:
  - It reads `crates/*/src` and `apps/*/src`.
  - It blanks comments and string and char literals, and measures a function from its `fn` line to its
    closing brace.
  - It excludes the generated `case_tables.rs`, test files (`tests.rs`, `*_tests.rs`, anything under a
    `tests/` directory), the functions inside `#[cfg(test)]` modules, and the `#[cfg(test)] fn samples`.
  - On `cc81e245` it reproduces every v0.2.1 figure: 264 files, 15 files over 1,000 lines, 19 functions
    over 150, 62 `use super::*;`.
  - Unlike the gate (`tools/rusekit/arch/size_scan.py`), it does not exempt `EmacsProfile::new` or
    `input/set_cmd/table.rs`. So its counts are one function and one file higher than `gov ratchet`'s.
- Path freshness comes from a second throwaway script with the v0.2.1 scope: every `apps/…`, `crates/…`,
  `tools/…` and `fuzz/…` path, `path:line` and `path::name` reference in `docs/` (without `docs/reviews/`),
  `spec/`, `AGENTS.md`, `CONTRIBUTING.md` and `README.md`.
- Parity figures come from `parity_compare` at `c62303c0`. For `cc81e245` they are derived from the corpus
  and the allowed-divergence list, not run: the comparison fails on any unlisted divergence and on any listed
  fixture that verifies, so when it passes, the list is exactly the divergent set.
- `llm` and `mixed` axes: assessed by reading the code at the cited lines.
- `manual` axes: marked **needs maintainer**, with what to look at.
- A verdict without evidence is not a pass.
- Security-relevant findings are withheld: their rows say only "details reported privately". This
  report has none.
- "Moved" compares with `docs/reviews/2026-11-16-v0.2.1.md`. "new" means the axis was not in that report.

     that no open issue holds can stay on it for good.
