**Top risks**

1. **The VT grid's root-cause defect is in vte 0.15, and the first property that found it merged while it
   was still flaky.**
   - The defect: when `vte::Parser::advance` ends inside a UTF-8 sequence, vte completes the char in the
     next call from a 4-byte window. If that window does not end on a char boundary, vte skips every byte
     after the char. A split `é` followed by LF loses the LF. A completed U+0080..=U+009F is also printed,
     where one feed executes it as C1 (#1765). 0.15.0 is the newest vte release (`Cargo.lock:1164-1165`), so
     no bump fixes it.
   - The fix: ruse now feeds vte through `term_grid::ChunkParser` (`term_grid.rs:694-768`). It holds back an
     incomplete UTF-8 tail (at most 3 bytes) and puts it in front of the next read, so vte's partial path
     is never taken. The drain loop, the fuzz entry (`fuzz_entry.rs:15`) and the tests all feed through it.
   - The canary `raw_vte_drops_the_byte_after_a_split_char` (`term_grid.rs:796-812`) asserts the vte
     defect. It fails once vte fixes it, and then the carry can go. The upstream report has not been filed.
     That is the maintainer's decision (Open questions 2).
   - The flake: #1764 merged the property `chunk_split_is_equivalent_to_one_feed` at 11:24 UTC with a
     `prop_assume!` that skipped inputs holding a byte in 0x80..=0x9F. It also quarantined the
     counterexample `split_utf8_then_lone_c1_matches_one_feed` under #1750 (a lone C1 byte), which has a
     different cause. The property still failed on other inputs. It failed intermittently on `main` and
     blocked local pre-push runs until #1766 merged at 11:53. The main push run for `e89495ca` happened to
     pass.
   - In ruse, only the PTY drain (with its fuzz entry and tests) feeds vte, and all of it now goes through
     `ChunkParser`.
2. **The terminal design doc is now wrong about the PTY in three places.** `docs/design/terminal.md:29-41`
   shows an unbounded `mpsc` and says "`Drop` hangs up (`SIGHUP`), reaps (`waitpid`), and joins the
   reader". Since #1758, `Drop` stops the reader first through a wake pipe, closes the master, waits 500 ms
   after SIGHUP, then sends SIGKILL and waits up to 2 s (`pty.rs:246-270`). Since #1759 the channel holds 64
   chunks and a drain tick has a budget (`term_buffer.rs:16-35`). `:50` says `Terminal::drain` "feeds raw
   PTY bytes straight into the `vte` parser". Since #1766 it goes through `ChunkParser`. `spec/PRD.yaml:910`
   (F-011 `trace.verify`) names `tests/integration/pty-unix`, which does not exist. The two new PTY suites
   are not linked from it.
3. **Most of the terminal work left v0.2.6 for v0.2.7, and v0.2.7 is now over its size.**
   - The milestone description reads: "Terminal output, keys, emulation and signals are reliable." 19
     follow-ups were filed while the fixes were written, 14 of them into v0.2.7. Several are parts of that
     promise:
     - keys: DECCKM and keypad modes (#1743), CTRL-\ CTRL-O (#1733), the modifyOtherKeys decoder (#1118);
     - emulation: erase, insert and delete splitting a wide glyph (#1734), the lone C1 byte (#1750), SGR 58
       in the semicolon form (#1742), the vttest transcript (#1752);
     - process: a child unreaped after a failed spawn (#1754), an exited child counted as running (#1738).
   - v0.2.7 now has 26 open issues (29 with the three review follow-ups #1772, #1774, #1775), against a target of about 20. v0.2.11 has 22 and v0.2.12 has 25
     (Open questions 1).
   - The VT conformance fixtures state what xterm's documentation says (`term_grid_conformance.rs:1-3`).
     They were not captured from a real terminal, unlike the nvim and Emacs fixtures. #1752 would add an
     oracle (vttest).
